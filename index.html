Which day. Day assignment follows the standard Ashkenaz selichot luach: selichot begin the Motzaei Shabbat before Rosh Hashanah, moving a week earlier when Rosh Hashanah falls on Monday or Tuesday. None are said on Shabbat or on Rosh Hashanah itself.

Timing. The Hebrew date rolls over at 7:00 PM local time as an approximation of nightfall, so a seder opened late at night is the one for that night and the following morning. Open the calendar to pick any day directly. Follow your congregation's practice wherever it differs.
